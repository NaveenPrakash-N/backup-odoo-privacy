Privacy Policy

Backup Odoo · Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Banibro Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you connect the Backup Odoo integration to your Google account to store Odoo database backups in Google Drive.

1. Information and access

When you authorize Backup Odoo, the integration receives the Google authorization credentials needed to perform the actions you approve. Depending on the permissions shown on Google's consent screen and the configuration you choose, it may access Google Drive to create, upload, or manage backup files. The integration is intended to use this access for the Odoo backup function.

Backup files may contain information from your Odoo database, including business, customer, employee, transaction, or other records stored in that database. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to back up that information.

2. How information is used

We use the access described above to connect Odoo with Google Drive and to carry out backup operations that you configure or initiate. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell Google user data.

3. Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in the Google Drive account and location selected or configured by the user. Access to those files is also governed by that Google account's sharing settings. We do not intentionally make backup files public or share them with third parties for their independent use.

OAuth credentials and tokens may be stored by the Odoo integration as required to maintain the connection. Administrators should protect access to their Odoo server, configuration, database, and backups.

4. Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until you or an authorized administrator delete them or until an automatic retention policy configured by your organization removes them. You can disconnect the Google account or revoke the integration's access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future Google Drive operations, but does not itself delete backup files already stored in Drive.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the integration's purpose to protect access to information. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use strong passwords, restrict administrator access, and protect your Odoo and Google accounts.

6. Third-party services

The integration uses Google services, including Google Drive and Google OAuth. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's terms and privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's privacy

Backup Odoo is a business software integration and is not intended for children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the integration or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published at this same URL with an updated effective date.
